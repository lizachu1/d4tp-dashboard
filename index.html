2027-2028 State Policy Agenda — Five Priorities

Strengthening California's Cradle-to-Career Data Infrastructure. Five priorities organized along the cradle-to-career continuum. Developed through the 2026 reflection survey, listening sessions, and October retreats.

1 Strengthen Early Learning & Care Data
  • Invest in a phased statewide ECIDS integrating data across state agencies
  • Establish an Early Learning & Care Data Task Force within C2C to prioritize data elements
  • Improve early childhood educator workforce data on preparation, retention, and workforce planning
Workgroup: ECE
2 Expand TK-12 Data Collection & Transparency
  • Strengthen educator workforce data via CTC and incorporate TAMO data into C2C
  • Expand multilingual learner data, including AB 2555 and Seal of Biliteracy records
  • Restore disaggregated A-G course access reporting to identify student disparities
Workgroup: TK-12
3 Improve Higher Ed Data & Pathways Transparency
  • Expand dual enrollment data and public reporting through C2C
  • Strengthen financial aid, student borrowing, and debt transparency with disaggregated data
Workgroup: HED/WF
4 Advance Workforce Data & Insights
  • Advance legislation to expand enhanced wage records and employment information
  • Expand workforce outcomes in C2C — credential pathways, apprenticeship earnings, employment data
Workgroup: HED/WF
5 Strengthen Statewide Data Infrastructure & Public Access
  • Secure ongoing funding for C2C operations, Jobs Hub, Research Gateway, and Workforce Pell
  • Expand longitudinal reporting linking college/career readiness to postsecondary outcomes
Workgroup: Cross-cutting
